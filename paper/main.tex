\begin{abstract}
We present a self-contained group-theoretic investigation resolving the mechanical degrees of freedom, vibrational representations, and optical selection rules of high-symmetry molecular systems. Treating the dihedral group $D_{6h}$ (exemplified by benzene, $\text{C}_6\text{H}_6$) and the full icosahedral point group $Y_h$ (represented by buckminsterfullerene, $\text{C}_{60}$), we construct the respective character tables from first principles and reduce the total mechanical representations ($\Gamma_{3N}$) via the projection operator formalism. We demonstrate how the $3N-6=174$ vibrational modes of $\text{C}_{60}$ collapse into only 4 infrared-active ($T_{1u}$) and 10 Raman-active ($A_g + H_g$) fundamentals under the mutual exclusion rule for centrosymmetric groups. Furthermore, the splitting of degenerate representations under progressive symmetry descent and its implications for dynamic Jahn--Teller instabilities are derived.
\end{abstract}

\section{Conclusion}
We have developed a comprehensive group-theoretic analysis resolving $D_{6h}$ and $Y_h$ molecular systems. The analytical framework developed in Refs.~\cite{Ramadevi2019, Dresselhaus2008} provides an exact description of the physical dynamics of complex macromolecules without requiring empirical parameterization. The companion open-source 3D visualization platform enables interactive real-time inspection of the complete 120-operation $Y_h$ manifold.
